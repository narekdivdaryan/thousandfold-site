Privacy

Thousandfold · thousandfold.online · last updated 18 November 2026

Playing without signing in

Your progress is kept only in your own browser (its local storage). Nothing about you is sent anywhere. Thousandfold has no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. It keeps your progress in your Google account so you can carry on from any device. If you sign in:

  • What we keep: your progress in the game (battles won, troops, upgrades and settings), when it was saved, and the account id that Google sign-in gives us.
  • What Google shares with us: your name, email address and profile picture, as with any "Sign in with Google". The game only shows your first name, on its menu.
  • Where it is kept: Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), on Google's servers in Europe.
  • Who can see it: only you. Each save can be read or changed only by the account it belongs to. We never sell or share it.
  • Why: only to keep and load your progress.

Removing it

On the game's menu, press Delete cloud save while signed in. That deletes your saved progress and your sign-in record at once. Your browser keeps its own copy of your progress until you clear the site's data. You can also remove Thousandfold's access in your Google account under Security, "Third-party apps and services".

Sending feedback

The Feedback button sends us what you write, any photo you add, and (if you leave it ticked) a picture of the battlefield as it was. It also sends the game's version, the screen and battle you were on, your browser and system (for example "Chrome on macOS") and your window's size, so we can find the bug. If you are signed in, it says which account sent it. Only the game's owner can read feedback; it is kept in Google Firebase in Europe, and deleted on request.

Fonts

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for them.
